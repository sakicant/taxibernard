FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book a Vodice transfer?

Advance booking is recommended to guarantee availability, especially during peak summer season. You can book directly through the contact page.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

The price is per vehicle, not per person. It covers up to 4 passengers and luggage at no extra cost, with a small additional fee for 5 or 6 passengers.

Can I book a one-way or round trip ride?

Both options are available. Just let Bernard know your plans when booking, and he'll arrange the pickup time that works for you.

Do you offer hotel or address pickup?

Yes, Bernard will pick you up directly from your hotel, apartment, or any address in Vodice, no need to meet at a central location.

Can I make a stop along the way?

Short stops can usually be arranged, just mention it when booking so Bernard can plan the route and timing accordingly.

What if my destination isn't listed?

No problem, contact Bernard directly through the contact page with your destination for a custom price quote.

Do you accept card payments?

Yes, card payments are accepted.
