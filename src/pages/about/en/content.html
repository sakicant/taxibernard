The story behind your driver in Vodice.

Bernard's Story

From the Road to the Driver's Seat

Bernard has been running his own taxi service in Vodice since 2024. Before getting behind the wheel professionally, he spent years on the road as a traveling sales representative, and later worked in freight forwarding and logistics, racking up countless kilometers and learning the region's roads long before taxi driving ever crossed his mind.

Languages

Speaking Your Language

Language is rarely a barrier with Bernard. He speaks Croatian and English fluently, and is also able to communicate in Serbian, Montenegrin, and Bosnian, useful for the wide range of visitors who pass through Vodice each summer.

Bernard's Vehicle

A Renault Espace, Ready for the Road

Bernard drives a comfortable Renault Espace, equipped with dual zone air conditioning and seating for up to six passengers plus the driver. For larger luggage, an extra suitcase can be arranged on request, and for families traveling with young children, a child seat and booster seat are available as part of the service.

With Bernard's experienced driving and the comfort of the Espace, you can count on getting to your destination safely and on time.
