3. Payment Policy

3.1. Advance payment is required to confirm every booking. For transfers priced over €20, the deposit is 20% of the total fare (minimum €20). Transfers priced at €20 or below require full advance payment.

3.2. The Customer may choose to pay the full fare in advance instead of the deposit, regardless of price.

3.3. The remaining balance is due to the driver at the time of the transfer, in cash or by card.

3.4. Accepted payment methods for the advance payment are bank transfer (SEPA) or credit/debit card. Payment instructions are sent by email after the booking request is received.

4. Cancellation and Refund Policy

4.1. Cancellations must be submitted in writing, by email.

4.2. The following refund policy applies:

  • More than 72 hours before the scheduled pickup: full refund for bank transfer payments; full refund minus a €10 processing fee for card payments.
  • 72 hours or less before the scheduled pickup: no refund.
  • No-show: no refund.

4.3. The €10 deduction on card payments covers non-recoverable processing charges retained by the payment provider.

4.4. Rescheduling a booking to a different date or time before departure incurs no additional charge; the advance payment already made transfers fully to the new date.

4.5. If a booking is cancelled by the Service Provider, the Customer receives a full refund regardless of the payment method used.

5. No-Show Policy

5.1. If the Customer or Passenger does not appear at the agreed pickup location within 15 minutes of the agreed pickup time without prior notice, the transfer will be treated as a no-show and the booking cancelled. No refund applies.

5.2. In the case of airport pickups, the Service Provider will monitor flight arrival information and allow reasonable additional waiting time in the event of documented flight delays, at no extra charge.

5.3. Repeated no-shows or last-minute cancellations without notice may result in the Service Provider declining future booking requests from the same Customer.

6.3. If a change results in a higher fare, the additional advance payment must be paid before the change is confirmed. If a change results in a lower fare, the difference is refunded or deducted from the balance due on the day.
