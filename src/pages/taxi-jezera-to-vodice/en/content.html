Taxi Jezera to Vodice: Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a taxi from Jezera to Vodice?

A fixed €40 per car for up to 4 passengers, one way, luggage included. A return trip is €80.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

The price is per vehicle, not per person. It covers up to 4 passengers and luggage at no extra cost, with a small additional fee for 5 or 6 passengers.

Where do you pick up?

At your exact address in Jezera, whether a home, hotel or apartment. Send the address when you book and Bernard will confirm the meeting point.

How far in advance should I book?

Advance booking is recommended to guarantee availability, especially during peak summer season. For a last-minute ride, call or WhatsApp Bernard directly.
